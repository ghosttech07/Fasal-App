One farmer's season, and what FasalShield changes

The season begins

Ramesh sows 2 acres of gram.

Seeds, fertiliser, labour. Everything he saved this year is now in the ground, waiting for rain.

Then

The rain stops.

Day after day the sky stays clear. The soil cracks. The young plants start to wilt.

The loss

The crop is lost.

Months of work and every rupee he spent, gone in one dry spell. In Marathwada this happens again and again.

The old way

Help is slow, if it comes.

Claim forms. Waiting for an official to inspect the field. Months pass before any money arrives.

With FasalShield

A satellite watches his rain.

Every day it measures the rain on the small square of land around his field. Nobody has to visit.

The rain fell below the mark

₹3,600 reaches his UPI.

Within 24 hours, with an SMS showing the rain measured. He filled no form. He waited for no one.

And so

He can sow again.

The money buys seed for the next sowing. His family is protected, and so is his next season.

Questions

Who decides that the rain failed?

Nobody decides. Satellite rain data for your area is compared with the normal rain for that area. The limits are written in your cover before you pay.

Do I have to file a claim?

No. If the rain fails, the money is sent on its own, with an SMS that shows the rain measured.

How fast does the money come?

Within 24 hours of the phase ending, to the UPI ID on your account.

My own rain gauge shows different rain. What then?

The cover pays on the satellite reading for your area, the same for every farmer in it. That is how it can pay without a visit. A shower on one corner can be missed.

Where is it available?

For Rabi 2026-27: Latur, Beed, Dharashiv and Nanded. Anyone in India can sign in and add a field.
